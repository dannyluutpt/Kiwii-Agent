# Marketing

What goes out to customers, built from the production showcase so nothing here costs a run.

- `promo/` the 30-second launch film as one page; `node marketing/promo/render.mjs` renders it.
- `social/` eight posts, each a showcase picture over its real prompt; `bun marketing/social/posts.ts`
  then `node marketing/social/build.mjs`.
- `out/` the rendered files.
- `content.md` captions, a two-week calendar and what still needs real runs (Vietnamese).

The strategy behind it is `docs/amberpix/go-to-market.md`.
